Senior Product Manager — Payments (Remote, NL)

infrastructure for European marketplaces.

The role

customers every week and turn data into decisions.

You bring
- 6+ years of product management experience, of which at least 2 in payments or fintech.
- Hands-on experience with SQL and product analytics tools like Amplitude.
- A bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.
- Excellent English. Dutch is a plus.

We offer
- A fully remote role within the Netherlands; the Amsterdam office is always open.
- A salary of up to €85,000 per year, plus stock options.
- 40 hours per week, with a 4x9 option.
- A permanent contract after a 1-year first contract.
